# Chrome Web Store listing: Park Lens 1.0.0

Draft text and answers for the Chrome Web Store Developer Dashboard. Assets in this folder are generated by `npm run store:assets`.

## Store listing

**Name:** Park Lens

**Summary** (manifest description, 132 characters max):
> See city parking near the place you're viewing in Google Maps. Covers Kitchener and Waterloo, ON.

**Category:** Travel

**Language:** English (Canada)

**Description:**

> Park Lens shows city-run parking near the place you're looking at in Google Maps, in Chrome's side panel.
>
> Open a place, or directions to one, in Google Maps, then click the Park Lens icon to see nearby city lots, garages and street parking in Kitchener and Waterloo, Ontario.
>
> For each option you'll see:
> • an approximate walk time to your destination
> • whether it fits your stay, or when you'd need to move (time limits, permit-only hours, closing times)
> • the estimated cost for your stay, using city rates
>
> Choose how long you're staying and when you'll arrive (now or later this week) to compare options for that visit. A general note summarizes each city's street parking rules, such as time limits, overnight parking and snow events.
>
> Good to know:
> • Only city-run parking in Kitchener and Waterloo, ON is included.
> • Walk times are straight-line estimates, and costs are estimates. Rates and rules come from the cities' open data and parking web pages and can change, so always check posted signs.
> • Some downtown Kitchener street segments are marked "unverified" and kept separate from confirmed options.
>
> Privacy: Park Lens reads only the address of Google Maps tabs, runs entirely on your device, makes no network requests, and collects no personal data.
>
> Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence - The Corporation of the City of Kitchener. Contains information provided by the City of Waterloo under licence.
>
> Park Lens is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google, the City of Kitchener or the City of Waterloo.

## Privacy practices tab

**Single purpose:**
> Shows city-run parking options, with walk times, costs and time limits, near the place the user is viewing in Google Maps.

**Permission justifications:**

| Permission | Justification |
|---|---|
| `sidePanel` | Park Lens displays its parking results in Chrome's side panel next to Google Maps. |
| Host permission `https://www.google.com/maps*`, `https://www.google.ca/maps*` | Lets the extension read the URL of Google Maps tabs to find the destination's name and coordinates. It is limited to Google Maps pages; no other site's URL or content is accessible, and nothing is injected into the page. |

**Remote code:** No, the extension does not use remote code. All code and data are bundled in the package.

**Data usage:** the extension collects none of the listed data types. Answers for each category:
- Personally identifiable information, health information, financial and payment information, authentication information, personal communications, location, web history, user activity, website content: **not collected**.

  The Google Maps URL is read only to compute results locally and is never stored or transmitted. Only the chosen stay length is saved, locally on the device.
- Certify: not sold to third parties; not used or transferred for purposes unrelated to the single purpose; not used for creditworthiness or lending.
